# 100 new poses for the Leap Motion, with reference joint ranges

Written 7 Nov 2026 by `scripts/leap/pose_catalogue.py`; the same data is `protocols/pose_catalogue_100.csv`. Gates: `GATES_pose_catalogue.md`.

## What this is

One hundred static hand poses that are not in the database yet, chosen so the Ultraleap IR 170 can see them (camera only, bare hand), each with an approximate reference range for every angle the camera gives: finger MCP flexion and abduction, PIP, DIP, thumb MCP and IP, wrist flexion and deviation. The purpose is the test a colleague proposed: record new poses and see whether the measured angles satisfy the constraint tables. A range here is what a held pose should look like, not a pass/fail limit: a recording inside it looks like the pose, one outside it is worth a look.

## How to read the ranges

Every pose is written as digit states, and every state has one template range, so the numbers are consistent across the catalogue. Angles are the camera's (`docs/joint_frames.md`): flexion positive toward the palm, abduction positive toward the thumb side, wrist flexion positive toward the palm, wrist deviation positive toward the thumb (radial). The thumb's TMC pair is not comparable across sources (`docs/tmc_estimate.md`), so the thumb carries its MCP and IP ranges and the TMC in words.

| state | wrist flexion | wrist deviation |
|---|---|---|
| neutral | -15 to 15 | -10 to 10 |

| radial | -15 to 15 | 10 to 20 |

Every template lies inside this envelope, the widest values of the constraint tables compiled on 7 Nov 2026 (Dragusanu 2025 Table I, Isaac 2021 Table 1, Ingram 2008 Table 1, Chen 2013 Table 6 which is Cobos 2009 Table 5, Jia 2021 Table 1, and the MyoHand model):

| angle | envelope | where the bound comes from |
|---|---|---|
| MCP_fe | -40 to 90 | Isaac -40 hyperextension; 90 flexion in Isaac, Chen and Jia |
| MCP_aa | -30 to 30 | Chen 60 total, Jia 0 to 60, so plus or minus 30 |
| PIP | -5 to 135 | Chen little PIP 135, Isaac 130; MyoHand stops at 90 and is the tightest |
| DIP | -30 to 90 | Isaac -30 to 90 |
| T_MCP_fe | -30 to 80 | Ingram observed -22; Isaac and Dragusanu 80 |
| T_IP | -45 to 90 | Ingram observed -43; Isaac 90 |
| WRIST_fe | -80 to 80 | Jia flexion 80, extension 70; Dragusanu plus or minus 90 is a model limit |
| WRIST_dev | -35 to 35 | Dragusanu plus or minus 35; Jia ulnar 30, radial 20 |

The templates are deliberately narrower than the envelope: a full curl is written as PIP 85 to 110 although the literature allows 135, because a held fist in the pilot takes read 90 to 100, and a straight finger is written as MCP -10 to 15 because the Leap rarely reports more hyperextension than that on a relaxed hand.

## What is excluded as already in the database

The ten paired poses of 2026-11-05 (open palm, fist, index point, thumbs up, peace, pinch, finger spread, thumb opposition, crossed fingers, bent wrist), the July seven (the same plus the three-finger W), the 23 mimed grasps of Set A (power and cylindrical wraps, hook, lateral, palmar, spherical, tip, tripod and the circular and prismatic precision family), and every step of the seven Set C sequences (one finger curled at a time, the adjacent pairs, the progressive fists, the thumb alone). The exclusion is by digit-state signature, so the ASL shapes that share one (S, U, H, W, F, O, M, N, 1, 2, 6, 9) are also left out; `tests/test_pose_catalogue.py` checks it. The professor's 102 tracker frames carry no pose names and are not matched here.

## Recording setup

Every pose: camera only, bare hand, palm toward the camera about 30 cm above it, held still 3 s. No gloves, no objects. One take is one held pose; record both hands if time allows, left first to match the pilot. The Leap column of the table says where the camera will struggle: a thumb hidden under the fingers is inferred, not seen, and curled fingertips can hide behind the proximal phalanx, so keep the palm square to the camera for those.
